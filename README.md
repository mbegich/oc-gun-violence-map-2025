# Orange County, CA gun violence incidents, 2025

Interactive map (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap) of 58 incidents dated Jan 1 to Dec 31, 2025 in Orange County, California cities, from the Gun Violence Archive (gunviolencearchive.org), retrieved Oct 17, 2026.

- `index.html`: the map (clustered markers, heat layer, Brea 5-mile ring)
- `oc_gun_violence_2025.csv`: the incident data, with geocoded coordinates and the method used
- `oc_gun_violence_2025.png`: static image

Locations were geocoded with the US Census geocoder and OpenStreetMap Nominatim. "Block of" addresses and intersections are approximate. GVA compiles incidents from police and media reports and may not be exhaustive.
